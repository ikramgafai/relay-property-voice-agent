# Relay v0.9.0 — speech output and guarded speech admission

Relay now answers in voice as well as text. Text remains the authoritative response; speech is
presentation only and can never change business state.

## Added

- **Realtime speech output.** `SpeechOutput` presents each first-delivery response through a
  provider-neutral `StreamingTTSProvider`, with one active and one pending output, a bounded
  500 ms frame buffer and lifecycle diagnostics.
- **ElevenLabs streaming TTS.** HTTP streaming adapter for `eleven_flash_v2_5` at `pcm_24000`, on
  the shared HTTP client, with safe timeout and failure mapping and no automatic retries.
- **LiveKit browser playback.** Agent audio is published as `relay-agent-audio` by the same backend
  participant that receives the caller's microphone, subscribable only by the caller. The browser
  attaches only that track and handles autoplay.
- **Audio lifecycle events.** `AGENT_AUDIO_OUTPUT_STARTED` / `AGENT_AUDIO_OUTPUT_ENDED`, sent after
  the text response and turn completion, carrying IDs, status and accepted audio duration only.
- **Spoken-reference filtering.** Operation UUIDs stay in the text response; speech says "The
  reference is available in the chat."
- **Replay-safe presentation.** Exact business replays are never re-spoken.
- **Guarded STT final admission.** A provider final stays provisional for 400 ms; speech resuming
  inside that window is coalesced into one turn, and admission fails closed (aborts) when an
  utterance may be incomplete.

## Changed

- **AssemblyAI endpointing** defaults raised from 128/1280 ms to 800/2000 ms after live speech split
  one sentence into two turns.
- LiveKit grants: the caller may subscribe (agent audio); the backend may publish one
  microphone-source track. Neither may publish data.

## Reliability

- TTS or sink failures leave text and committed business results intact; input keeps working.
- Interrupting a blocked LiveKit `capture_frame()` disables the sink rather than corrupting it.

## Validation

- Real-microphone 9-turn flagship: 9 provider finals, 9 admitted turns, 9/9 speech outputs
  completed, one incident created and updated in place, one linked handoff, 3 tool calls, no
  duplicate executions.
- 530 automated tests, strict mypy, Ruff; browser audio module tests run in CI.

## Not yet validated live

- Coalescing two provider finals into one application turn (covered by deterministic tests).
- Barge-in is not implemented; it is the next phase.
